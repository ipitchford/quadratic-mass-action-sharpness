# Citation audit

The final manuscript contains six bibliography entries, each cited in its text. Full query logs, source versions, inspected passages, comparisons, and limitations are in PRIOR_ART.md.

- Banaji–Feliu (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00285-026-02429-8: source-only quadratic definition, Theorem 5.5 upper bound, Remark 5.8 exact sharpness question. Its rank-one remark is not used.
- Banaji–Boros–Hofbauer (2024), DOI 10.1007/s11071-024-10068-1: existing planar chemical attainment, Remark 38. Used only for context; the present proof is independent.
- Bihan (2015), DOI 10.1016/j.jsc.2014.09.003: maximally positive circuit systems, methodological prior art.
- El Hilany (2017), DOI 10.1007/s00454-017-9897-4: circuit-support characterization, methodological prior art.
- Bihan–Dickenstein–Forsgård (2021), DOI 10.1007/s00208-021-02216-4: sharp Descartes bounds on circuit systems, methodological prior art.
- Phillipson–Rojas (2013), DOI 10.1090/conm/605/12111; inspected arXiv:1011.4128v5 (6 November 2012), Theorem 1.6 and its proof: the actual earlier maximal-root quadratic chain family. The explicit chemical bridge, including preservation of nonsingularity, is shown in the manuscript. Journal metadata was corroborated through published references and an author publication list; direct DOI browser resolution failed. No false claim of a retained local source-PDF hash is made.

Bibliographic existence does not establish chemical novelty. The bounded audit found no prior chemical realization of the matched family, but that absence is not a proof of historical priority.
